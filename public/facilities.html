Campus facilities

Everything you need to learn, practise, play and live well, all on one campus.

Smart Labs
01

Smart Labs

Practical learning is at the heart of every program. Our labs are updated regularly so students train on tools they will meet at work.

  • Computer labs with high-speed internet and current software
  • Electronics, physics and chemistry labs with guided experiments
  • Workshop and robotics area for mechanical projects
  • Lab assistants available during and after class hours
Central Library
02

Central Library

A quiet, well-stocked space for study and research, with over 60,000 books and access to digital journals.

  • Spacious reading halls and group-study rooms
  • Digital library with e-books and research journals
  • Extended hours during exam weeks
  • Study materials from teachers also available on the portal
Sports Complex
03

Sports Complex

Healthy bodies support healthy minds. Our sports facilities are open to every student, whether you play for fun or for the college team.

  • Cricket ground and athletics track
  • Indoor courts for badminton, table tennis and basketball
  • Fully equipped gymnasium
  • Coaching and inter-college tournaments every year
Hostels & Canteen
04

Hostels & Canteen

A safe, comfortable home away from home for students who travel from other parts of Telangana and India.

  • Separate hostels for boys and girls with 24/7 security
  • Hygienic, balanced meals in the campus canteen
  • Wardens and support staff on site round the clock
  • Study rooms, common areas and Wi-Fi in every block
