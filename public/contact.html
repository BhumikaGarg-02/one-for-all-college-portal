FAQ

Quick answers

How do I get my portal ID?

IDs are issued by the admin office after admission is confirmed. Students get an ID like STD0001 and teachers get one like TEA0001. There is no public sign-up.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the "Forgot password?" link on the login page. The admin office will reset it and give you a temporary password, which you will be asked to change when you sign in.

How can I apply for a program?

Send an enquiry using this form or visit the admissions desk during office hours. The team will explain eligibility, dates and documents.

Can I visit the campus before applying?

Yes. Campus visits are welcome from Monday to Saturday during office hours. Please send a short message first so we can arrange a guide.
