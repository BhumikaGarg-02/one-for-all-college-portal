About ONE FOR ALL

A Hyderabad college built on one idea: every learner, teacher and staff member deserves the same access to knowledge and opportunity.

Our Story

From one block to a full campus

ONE FOR ALL began in 1985 with three departments and 120 students. The founders wanted a college where a student from any background in Telangana could get a strong education close to home.

Four decades later, the campus is home to thousands of learners and hundreds of faculty. Our graduates lead teams in technology, healthcare, finance and public service across Hyderabad and beyond.

Our name is our promise: one community, and one set of opportunities for all.

What Guides Us

Vision, mission and values

Our Vision

To be a leading institution in Telangana, known for academic excellence, honest character and service to society.

Our Mission

To give every student quality teaching, modern facilities and personal guidance, so they graduate ready for work, study and life.

Our Values

Integrity, curiosity, respect and teamwork. We hold ourselves to these values in classrooms, labs and on the sports field.

Milestones

A journey of steady growth

Every milestone came from listening to students and faculty and building what they needed next. The ONE FOR ALL portal is the latest step, bringing academics and administration into one place.

  1. 1985 College founded with 3 departments
  2. 1998 Affiliated with the state university; new library block
  3. 2003 First postgraduate programs launched
  4. 2009 Autonomous status and research centre opened
  5. 2014 Placement cell and industry partnerships established
  6. 2018 Smart classrooms and digital campus launched
  7. 2026 ONE FOR ALL portal goes live
