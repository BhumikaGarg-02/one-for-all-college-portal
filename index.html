Our History

Four decades of shaping Hyderabad's future

Founded in 1985 with a single block and 120 students, the college has grown into one of the city's most trusted institutions. Our graduates now work across Hyderabad's technology, healthcare and research sectors and in cities around the world.

The ONE FOR ALL portal continues that journey by bringing academics, communication and administration together in one simple place.

  1. 1985 College founded with 3 departments
  2. 1998 Affiliated with the state university; new library block
  3. 2009 Autonomous status and research centre opened
  4. 2018 Smart classrooms and digital campus launched
  5. 2026 ONE FOR ALL portal goes live

Principal's Message

Education is a shared effort

Every student who walks through our gates carries the hopes of a family and the promise of a community. Our job is to make sure they leave with knowledge, character and the confidence to lead. This portal is one more step in making that journey open and transparent for everyone.
